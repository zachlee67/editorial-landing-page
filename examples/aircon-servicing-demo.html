What Makes Chemical Wash Different?

A standard service wipes the visible parts and checks gas pressure. Chemical wash goes further: the coil and evaporator are removed, soaked, and rinsed with a proper cleaning chemical, clearing out the grime a normal service never touches.

Just Need a Routine Check? General Servicing Covers That.

Not every visit needs a full chemical wash. General servicing covers filter cleaning, gas top-up, and a performance check for units that are cooling fine but due for routine maintenance.

  • Filter clean and external wipe-down
  • Gas pressure check and top-up if needed
  • Recommended every 3-4 months for daily-use units
