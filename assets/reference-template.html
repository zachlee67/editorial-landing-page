How Does Ultherapy Actually Work?

Ultherapy uses micro-focused ultrasound with visualization (MFU-V) to reach the SMAS, the same deep structural layer targeted in a surgical facelift, without a single cut. With DeepSEE™ real-time imaging, your doctor can actually see the layer being treated on screen, so energy goes exactly where it's needed and safely avoids bone and blood vessels.

It's the only non-surgical treatment FDA-cleared to lift skin on the neck, chin, and brow, and to improve lines and wrinkles on the décolletage.

How the Lift Actually Happens

01

Focused Energy Delivery

Ultrasound energy is delivered to precise depths, creating controlled thermal points at 60–70°C without touching the skin's surface.

02

Immediate Contraction

Existing collagen fibres denature and contract on contact, giving some patients a subtle immediate tightening effect.

03

Neocollagenesis

Your body's healing response builds fresh collagen over the following 2–3 months, producing a gradual, natural-looking lift.
